# Full Plan: Product Photo → Realistic Ad Video (sub-phase version)

## Rules for every sub-phase

1. Do one sub-phase at a time. Don't start the next until the pass check is met.
2. Run each test on all 10 test products. Make the set first: shiny metal, transparent glass, food, clothing, jewellery, text-heavy packaging, white product on white background, blurry photo, tiny photo, normal bottle/box.
3. Log every failure in `issues.md` with the date, the sub-phase, the product, what happened, and the fix.
4. Make a git tag after each pass (`v0a`, `v1c`, ...) so you can always roll back.

Core rule: the real product photo is never regenerated by AI. AI and FFmpeg only build the background, motion, voice and text around it.

## Phase 2: Ad brief (LLM)

|---|---|---|---|
| 2a | LLM adapter with one provider (Gemini free) | Returns text for a test prompt | Rate limits |

## Phase 4: Product image prep

| Step | Build | Test and pass | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4a | Background removal (rembg) | Cutout produced for all 10 | Slow on CPU |
| 4b | Mask cleanup: edge smoothing, remove halo | Zoom to 300%, no white fringe | Hair, fabric, and fine edges |
| 4c | Crop to product bounds, add padding | Product centered, nothing clipped | Shadows included in the mask by mistake |
| 4d | Upscale low-res products (Real-ESRGAN or Lanczos) | No visible blur at 1080 wide | Upscaling can distort text on packaging |
| 4e | Support gate: detect or let the user flag unsupported types | Glass, transparent, and white-on-white get a clear "not supported yet" | Decide your launch scope here |
| 4f | Place cutout on a 1080×1920 transparent canvas | Product is a consistent size and position | Very wide or very tall products |

## Phase 5: Background and scene (the hardest realism step)

| Step | Build | Test and pass | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5a | Studio gradient backgrounds (5 styles) | Product on gradient looks clean | Colour clash with the product |
| 5b | Placement rules: scale, ground line, margin | Product never floats or gets cut off | Different aspect ratios |
| 5c | Soft contact shadow and floor reflection | Product looks grounded | Shadow direction and hardness |
| 5d | Basic colour and brightness match between product and background | No obvious "pasted on" look | Product photo lighting vs background lighting |
| 5e | Category-matched backgrounds from Pexels (kitchen for food, desk for gadgets) | Background fits the category | Cluttered or wrong-mood photos |
| 5f | Generate 3 variants, auto-score them, pick the best | Winner beats a random pick in your review | Scoring is subjective, so use your own judgment to tune it |
